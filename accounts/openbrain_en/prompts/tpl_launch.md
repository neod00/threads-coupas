Type: launch (the first post introducing the app. Each app gets this only once. A demo video and screenshots are attached.)

Good solo-dev launch posts say what was built in the first line, add a short reason why, finish within a few lines, and ask for feedback.
Example (a different app): "made an app that only gives you routes in the shade. this summer was way too hot. tell me if anything's off"

Hook: one sentence saying what I built (app.name or what the app does). Do not open with a question about the reader's pain.
    e.g. "built a pdf editor that never uploads your file"

Body (3 to 7 short lines)
- First line: the app name (app.name) and what it does (app.one_liner, app.how) in plain words. Do not repeat the hook.
- Why I built it: if app.stories exists, one origin story exactly as written. If stories is empty, skip this line.
- One or two facts people wonder about before trying it (is it free, do you need to sign up or install anything).
- If there are limits, one honest line ("building it solo, so there are rough edges").
- Last line: ask for feedback on the app. e.g. "try it and tell me what sucks"
- A video and screenshots are attached, but do not write "see the video" or "check the screenshots".

reply_line: one sentence for the link reply. e.g. "You can try it here."
