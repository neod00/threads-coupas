Type: feature (one feature of the app. A screenshot of that feature or a short demo video is attached.)

This is not a manual. It is a quick "my pdf editor can do this" moment. Talk about one feature only.
The same feature comes back every couple of weeks, so the post must not read like the last one. The data has "angle": follow it.

angle = "show" (what the feature does)
Hook: one sentence about what this feature lets you do, told as news about my app.
    e.g. "my pdf editor finds phone numbers and redacts them in one go", "my pdf editor does signatures too"
Body (3 to 5 short lines)
- First line: the app name (app.name) and what this feature does. Use the app name only once.
- One or two lines on how it works, using only what feature.desc says.
- Last line: a short ask for feedback on this feature or the app.

angle = "use" (one situation where the feature is handy; feature.use is that situation, written by the maker)
Hook: my app doing that one job, in one sentence. Still about my app, not a question about the reader's pain.
    e.g. "my pdf editor pulls just the pages you need out of a long scan", "signed a lease pdf without printing it, in my own editor" is NOT ok
    (that claims i did it). Say what the app can do: "my pdf editor signs a lease without the print and scan dance"
Body (3 to 5 short lines)
- First line: the situation in feature.use, said as something that comes up in general, not as something that happened to me or to a user.
- Then the app name (app.name, once) and how the feature handles that situation, using only what feature.desc says.
- Do not list other situations and do not explain the whole feature again.
- Last line: a short ask, e.g. "what would you use it for?", "tell me where it falls short".

Both angles
- Do not open with a question about the reader's pain.
    Bad: "still deleting phone numbers from PDFs one by one?", "printing and scanning just to sign?"
- This feature already exists. Do not say when it was made or that it is new: no "today", "just added", "just rolled out", "new", "now".
- Do not invent behavior, formats or numbers that are not in feature.desc.
- The no-upload fact (app.how) is not needed in every post. Mention it only when the document in this post is sensitive
  (personal data, contracts, signatures), and then in one short line.
- Do not start the body with "PdfMonster lets you" every time, and do not use "Handy for" or "Handy when". Vary how the lines begin.
- A screenshot or video is attached, but do not write "see the screenshot".

reply_line: one sentence for the link reply, about this feature. e.g. "You can try it here.", "The signing tool is here."
