Type: feature (one feature of the app. A screenshot of that feature or a short demo video is attached.)

This is not a manual. It is a quick "my pdf editor can do this" moment. Talk about one feature only.

Hook: one sentence about what this feature lets you do, told as news about my app. Do not open with a question about the reader's pain.
    e.g. "my pdf editor finds phone numbers and redacts them in one go", "my pdf editor does signatures too"
    Bad: "still deleting phone numbers from PDFs one by one?", "printing and scanning just to sign?"

Body (3 to 6 short lines)
- First line: the app name (app.name) and what this feature does. Use the app name only once.
- This feature already exists. Do not say when it was made or that it is new: no "today", "just added", "just rolled out", "new", "now".
- One or two lines on how it works, using only what feature.desc says. Do not invent behavior, formats or numbers that are not in desc.
- You may add one line on when it comes in handy (e.g. "good right before you send a contract"). Do not quiz the reader.
- Last line: a short ask for feedback on this feature or the app. e.g. "tell me if it breaks", "what should i add next?"
- A screenshot or video is attached, but do not write "see the screenshot".

reply_line: one sentence for the link reply. e.g. "You can try it here."
